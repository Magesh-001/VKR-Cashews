The 5-Stage VKR Quality Discipline

How five decades of experience guards every batch

01

Selection

Raw seed nuts carefully procured from verified growers across Panruti and certified global orchards.

02

Processing

Gentle steam roasting, precision decortication, and artisanal peeling that maintains whole kernel integrity.

03

Quality Check

Hand sorting across strict size matrices (W180–W400), moisture control, and optical inspection.

04

Packaging

Vacuum sealed nitrogen-flushed tins and airtight foil barrier poly bags to lock in fresh crunch.

05

To Customer

Direct dispatch from Neyveli bazaar counter or VeerasinganKuppam factory to homes & businesses nationwide.
