Expert Buyer's Knowledge

Understanding Cashew Grading & Size Scale

In international cashew grading, the letter “W” stands for “White Whole” and the subsequent numeral signifies the average kernel count per pound (453.6 grams). Smaller numbers indicate larger, rarer kernels.

Largest

W-180

170 – 180 Kernels / lb

The rarest 2-3% of every harvest. Monumental size, highest oil balance, pristine crunch. Reserved for VIP gifting.

Large

W-240

220 – 240 Kernels / lb

Imposing visual appeal with accessible bulk supply. Ideal for elite bakeries and boutique confectionery packaging.

Benchmark

W-320

300 – 320 Kernels / lb

The world's standard cashew grade. Balances price and stature perfectly for roasting, salting, and gourmet snack brands.

Economy

W-400

380 – 400 Kernels / lb

Smaller full whole kernels providing high count per kilogram. Superior economy without breaking or crushing the whole kernel.
