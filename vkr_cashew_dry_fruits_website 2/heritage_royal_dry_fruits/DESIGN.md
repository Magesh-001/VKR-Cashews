## Brand & Style

The brand identity embodies five decades of royal artisanal heritage, uncompromising purity, and refined Indian culinary luxury. Designed for connoisseurs of gourmet dry fruits, affluent gift-givers, and institutional clientele seeking authentic farm-to-table perfection, the visual language merges classic imperial craftsmanship with editorial modernity.

The design movement balances **Tactile Heritage Luxury** and **Warm Editorial Minimalism**:
- Rich, warm canvas foundations (cream and unbleached parchment) inspired by vintage harvest ledgers and gilded botanical cataloguing.
- Delicate metallic gold hairline borders, regal filigree framing, and commemorative insignia (celebrating 50 Years: 1976–2026).
- Deep, resonant earthy tones honoring natural kernel roasts and orchard canopies, avoiding artificial brightness or cartoonish graphic tropes.
- Tactile, packaging-inspired card elevations featuring subtle letterpress debossing, gold foil hot-stamps, and archival typographic layouts.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from royal harvests, golden roasting vats, and sun-drenched cashew orchards:

- **Primary (`#2B1810` - Deep Walnut Brown):** Represents grounded legacy, roasted nut profiles, and stately authority. Serves as the primary structural color for headers, dominant surfaces, and high-emphasis packaging borders.
- **Secondary (`#C89D42` - Warm Golden Ochre):** Evokes celebratory gold-foil stampings, brass artisanal roasting drums, and jubilee prestige. Employed for call-to-action treatments, decorative hairline strokes, badges, and key accents.
- **Tertiary (`#3A5A40` - Botanical Orchard Green):** Anchors the brand's natural provenance, fresh harvest canopy, and purity. Used sparingly for organic certifications, quality tags, and secondary action confirmations.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#FAF7F2` - Ivory Cream):** A soft, comforting, non-glare surface that simulates handmade textured paper and ivory silk, supported by `#EFE8DD` (Soft Beige) for subtle structural section breaks.

Text contrast strictly enforces WCAG AAA compliance, pairing deep walnut on ivory backgrounds and warm metallic gold on dark walnut containers.

## Typography

Typography establishes an aristocratic balance between archival prestige and razor-sharp digital commerce clarity:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Imparts old-world luxury, graceful transitional contrast, and editorial elegance. Titles evoke royal warrants, heritage trade ledgers, and boutique confectionery packaging.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Provides supreme legibility, modern balance, and gentle warmth that prevents the interface from feeling stiff or archaic.
- **Kernel Grades & Product Metrics (`grade-display` / uppercase labels):** Cashew grades (e.g., *W-180 King Size*, *W-240 Premium Jumbo*, *JH Split*) are treated as royal appellations, set with deliberate letter spacing and balanced high-contrast weight.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy champions editorial breathing room and structured rhythm, echoing high-end gift catalogs and boutique store displays:

- **Grid Architecture:** Desktop views follow a 12-column layout with generous gutters (`1.5rem`) and broad frame margins (`3rem`) to replicate the borders of gilded framed folios. Mobile compresses cleanly into a single- or two-column staggered grid with `1.25rem` margins.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Density is intentionally generous. Luxury is communicated through emptiness; product cards and heritage storytelling segments are framed with ample margins rather than crammed edge-to-edge.
- **Responsive Stacking:** Product grids adapt from 4 columns on wide displays (`>= 1280px`), to 3 columns on desktop (`>= 1024px`), 2 columns on tablet (`>= 768px`), and a structured vertical flow or side-peeking carousel on mobile viewports.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile, physical packaging metaphors rather than synthetic dropshadows:

- **Warm Ambient Shadows:** Shadows utilize walnut pigments instead of dead charcoal (`rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.08)` to `rgba(43, 24, 16, 0.14)`) with soft, widespread blur radii (16px to 32px), creating a gentle lift as if resting on velvet or cream linen.
- **Gold Foil Filigree & Hairline Strokes:** Rather than heavy elevation tiers, card separations rely on 1px crisp borders tinted in warm ochre or burnished gold (`#C89D42` at 30% to 60% opacity).
- **Embossed & Debossed Accents:** Subtle dual-line internal borders (a 1px gold border inset by 3px within cards) evoke luxury tin boxes, traditional wooden gift boxes, and embossed golden seal crests.
- **Surface Layering:** Surfaces step gracefully from `#FAF7F2` (base ivory background) to `#FFFFFF` (elevated product cards) to `#2B1810` (statement heritage sections and commemorative footers).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts **Soft Geometric Tailoring (`roundedness: 1`)** to maintain an architectural, time-tested heritage stature:

- Corner radii remain subtle (4px default for controls, 8px for cards, 12px for modal framing). Overly round or bubble geometries are strictly avoided to preserve imperial prestige.
- **Heritage Badges & Insignia:** Circular or scalloped geometric seals, octagonal corner insets, and ornamental bracket motifs are used for jubilee badges (e.g., *1976–2026 / 50th Year Golden Jubilee*).
- **Dividers:** Defined by center-embellished hairline rules featuring leaf or botanical leaf flourishes in tertiary green or metallic ochre.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Buttons:** Rich Walnut Brown (`#2B1810`) fill with ivory bold typography, surrounded by an interior or outer 1px warm gold border (`#C89D42`). Hover state shifts background to a radiant roasted bronze with an understated gold glow.
- **Secondary Buttons:** Ivory or soft beige background bounded by a sharp 1.5px metallic gold border; text in Walnut Brown with subtle letter spacing.
- **Tertiary Buttons:** Text-only in botanical green or walnut brown, paired with a persistent 1px underline that expands smoothly on hover.

### Product & Gift Box Cards
- Constructed with crisp ivory backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`), inset 1px gold hairline borders (`#C89D42` at 40%), and subtle 8px corner radii.
- Features dedicated top badges for nut grading (e.g., "GRADE W-180" in Playfair Display), botanical purity tags ("100% NATURALLY DRIED"), and subtle price/weight tags set in tabular numerals.

### Heritage & Jubilee Badges
- Commemorative 50-year jubilee elements feature concentric circular framing with embossed golden foil gradients (`#DFB15B` to `#C89D42`), enclosing deep walnut typography that commemorates five decades of mastery.

### Chips & Grade Selectors
- Pill-free, soft-cornered rectangles (4px radius). Unselected states feature soft beige backgrounds (`#EFE8DD`) with walnut text. Selected states feature deep walnut fill with gold foil border outlines and bold contrasting text.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- Crisp ivory fields with 1px border (`#C89D42` at 35%). When focused, borders shift to solid `#C89D42` with an ambient warm nut shadow. Floating labels employ uppercase tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square with softened 2px corners (checkboxes) and concentric circles (radios). Bordered in `#2B1810`; active selection fills with `#2B1810` alongside an interior gold checkmark or pip.
